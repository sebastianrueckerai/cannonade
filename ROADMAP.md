# Roadmap

Everything we want or might want for Cannonade: the game, its art, its design and the process that makes the art. A session pulls items by ID into its plan in [art/docs/sessions/](art/docs/sessions), refines them there, ships them, and ticks them in the same commit. Add new items at the end of their section. Never delete an item, strike it through with the reason. Keep no other TODO lists.

Tags: **must** is needed for the first demo. **spike** is a time-boxed experiment that answers a question. An art or process spike ends in an entry in [art/docs/process-log.md](art/docs/process-log.md), a game spike in a note on its item. **idea** is optional. **decide** is a choice Sebastian makes. **game** means the change lands in the game itself, outside [art/](art).

Sections A to H cover the art and the process that makes it, sections I to K the game code. The rules to build next are [rules v2](art/game/design/rules.md). The current plan is the highest-numbered file in [art/docs/sessions/](art/docs/sessions).
