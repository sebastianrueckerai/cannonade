# Inspirations

Sebastian's references, 11 October 2026. A capsule or header image means "I like the mood and the colours of the capsule", not the in-game graphics. A screenshot means the in-game look. Game names come from the file names, from Sebastian's corrections, or from the agent's reading. Rows marked unknown stay unknown, Sebastian does not want to label them, and every game here counts as a decent inspiration. What matters to him: a saturated but cosy palette and lighting, a readable grid, and the River Towns level of detail.

## What Sebastian said

- **River Towns**: a great combination of readable grid graphics, nice colours and the right level of detail. The strongest match.
- **Foundation**: nice colours and buildings with a nice level of detail.
- **Kingdoms and Castles**: looks a bit like what I want, but too stylised, too voxel-like, and not quite the right colours.
- **Flotsam**: does it better than Kingdoms and Castles.
- **Of Life and Land**: a bit too blocky, too low poly.
- **Corsair Cove**: great, just a bit too realistic and too detailed.
- **A Monster's Expedition**: I like the colours and mood.
- **Era**: age of sail is great for castles and the like, and it cannot hurt to dip into the middle ages where required. No constraint to modern forts.

The pattern: the capsule is a painting, whatever the game renders. The lettering is white or cream, large, clear at capsule size, on a quiet part of the painting, and its script carries the game's mood: cosy, storybook, formal. No extrusion, no bevel, no WordArt, at most a soft shadow or a thin outline. What to take for Grid Cannons: a painted scene with the fortress and a striped-sail ship large and simple, warm afternoon light, and a cheerful, clear, hand-lettered or storybook title in white or cream.

## What to take, agent's reading

- From River Towns: the grid that stays readable at every zoom, one saturated colour block per building with a roof, water as channels, the terrain colour under the grid, and the amount of detail per building.
- From Foundation: the ceiling of detail per building, and the timber and stone colours in warm light.
- From Nova Roma: how saturated roofs can be under bright light without turning loud.
- From Flotsam: the cheerful palette, outlines that keep small things readable.
- From Kingdoms and Castles: the rounded fortress forms and the scale of walls against houses, not the voxel look.
- From Corsair Cove: the coastal fortress mood and the water, at a third of the detail.
- Not from of Life and Land: the blocky terrain and the low vertex count per building.

## A Monster's Expedition

Twelve images from the Chickadee Games project page, https://www.chickadeegames.com/project_monstersexpedition.html, saved as `monsters-expedition-<name>.jpg`: five biome paintings, key art, concept notes, mockups, the monster, the summer girl, the album and the exhibits. Capsule rule applies: colours and mood, not the in-game graphics.

What to take, agent's reading: the islands are small, have a visible checker grid on the land, sit in a teal sea that fades to cream at the horizon, and are wrapped in soft fog. Few objects per island, every one a big readable shape. The mockups image is close to a world anchor for a fortress on a tile island. The biome paintings give five palettes on the same shapes: sand, snow, kelp, tropical, spring.
