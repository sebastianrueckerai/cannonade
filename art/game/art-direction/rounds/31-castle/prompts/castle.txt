Paint it as a concept artist's painted sketch sheet for a cozy, bright strategy game: loose hand-painted brushwork with visible strokes, soft painted light from a warm sunny afternoon coming from the front right, cheerful bright colours, a warm light cream paper background with a soft painted shadow under each object. It is a painting, not a 3D render, not low poly, not a game screenshot. Cozy and friendly like a storybook toy, never grim. No people, no text, no labels, no numbers, no letters, no watermark.

The sheet shows four different designs for one building, side by side from left to right: the castle of a toy fortress, the prize the walls are built around, much wider than it is tall. Each design stands on its own square tile of bright green grass and fills most of it, seen in a three-quarter view from slightly above. Each design is a whole castle made of separate modules in warm cream stone, plain smooth walls with no stones drawn on them: a square curtain wall with crenellated battlements on top, one tower at each of the four corners, a small gatehouse with a wooden door in the front wall, and a keep in the middle rising above the curtain walls. Every tower and the keep are built in two or three distinct storeys, each storey slightly narrower than the one below it, with a clear horizontal band between storeys, so each storey reads as a separate drum that could crumble away on its own. The team colour is a plain royal blue: on every design the tower roofs, the keep roof and the flags are royal blue, and nothing else is blue. Design 1: the classic castle, four round corner towers with conical royal blue roofs, a square keep with battlements and one royal blue flag. Design 2: the gatehouse castle, its front gatehouse the tallest part with two big square towers and a long royal blue banner hanging between them, small round towers on the back corners, a slim keep behind. Design 3: the chunky toy castle, everything square and chunky, one low storey of curtain wall, four square corner towers with steep royal blue pyramid roofs, a wide keep with battlements. Design 4: the courtyard castle, a low curtain wall, one tall round tower with a conical royal blue roof, a smaller square keep with a royal blue banner, a tiny gate.
