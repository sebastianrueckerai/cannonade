# Round 38: menu, win and lose as versions of the build theme

Source: the chiptune build theme of round 36 from Stable Audio 2.5, 90 s, 0 words in the Whisper check,
chosen by its numbers because no one had listened yet. The menu version remixes the whole clip, the win
and the lose version remix its first 16 s with a fade, through fal-ai/ace-step/audio-to-audio in remix
mode. The remix keeps the material of the source and takes the new tags.

- menu: the build theme slower and lighter, a music box over the chip lead.
- win: the fanfare, fast, bright, major, a victory jingle.
- lose: slow, minor, sparse, a defeat jingle.
