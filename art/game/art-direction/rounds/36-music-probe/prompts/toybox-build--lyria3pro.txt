Instrumental only, no vocals, no singing, no lyrics. Toy-box folk orchestra. Tin whistle and fiddle lead, accordion chords, marimba and glockenspiel, pizzicato strings, woodblock and tambourine, a bodhran hand drum. Acoustic, warm, wooden, like a painted toy harbour. Background music for the build phase of a cheerful castle board game: the player stacks painted wooden wall pieces on a tile board, friendly and quick, sunlit toy fortress by the sea. Genre: playful game loop. Tempo: 108 BPM. Key: G major. Mood: calm, bouncy, curious, light. Arrangement: a steady loop of a catchy eight-bar melody with a second eight bars that answers it, a light counter-melody, no big build-up, no breakdown, no ending, constant energy so it loops. About 90 seconds long.
