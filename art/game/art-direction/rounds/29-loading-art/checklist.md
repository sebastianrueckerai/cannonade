# Checklist, round 29

Sebastian's key-art rules of [round 10](../10-duel-checked/checklist.md) that apply to a single picture, plus the lines a loading screen needs. A picture is shown only when it passes every line, each checked in a magnified crop. A line that cannot be checked counts as a fail.

| # | rule | from | how it is checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Painterly 2D in the cover art's soft colours, not oversaturated, not the game's render look | D24, round 10 line 12 | whole frame beside the cover |
| 2 | No text, letters, numbers or watermark | round 10 line 13 | whole frame |
| 3 | No people | round 11 prompts | whole frame |
| 4 | At most one gun fires, with its flash and smoke at the muzzle and its ball on the barrel's line. The flak's burst counts as its one shot | round 10 lines 3 and 4 | the shot at 2x |
| 5 | Every cannon is complete, one barrel on one carriage, not floating, not pasted, not buried in a wall | round 10 line 6 | each gun at 2x |
| 6 | Every gun stands on the courtyard floor inside the walls, never on a wall, a wall walk, a tower, a roof or a house | round 10 line 5 | each gun at 2x |
| 7 | Cannon barrels raised to about 45 degrees. The mortar points steeply up, the flak aims up | round 10 line 7 | each gun at 2x |
| 8 | Castle walls straight and along the grid. Round corner towers are allowed | round 10 line 10b | whole frame |
| 9 | Where land shows, the tile grid is painted in the castles' perspective | round 10 line 10 | whole frame |
| 10 | Not too busy, nothing too small, not barren | round 10 lines 11 and 11b | whole frame |
| 11 | The bottom right corner is calm where the loading bar sits, the lowest sixth across the right third: no important subject there | D65 | the bar's real box drawn on the frame |
| 12 | No siege gun, nothing bigger than the 2x2 heavy gun | D64 | each gun |
| 13 | Ideas 5 to 13: recognisably the cover's near castle, a square keep with a timber lookout under an orange pyramid roof and a red flag | the brief | castle crop beside the cover's |
