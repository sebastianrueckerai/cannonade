You are an independent critic for a cosy low-poly browser strategy game in the spirit of Rampart: castles on a tile grid shoot at each other with cannons. These 5 images are painted concept art for its loading screen, in the style of the game's painted cover art. A loading bar panel covers the bottom right corner of each image. Your job is faults, not taste.

The owner's checklist, every line must pass:
1. Painterly 2D in the cover art's soft colours, not oversaturated, not the game's render look
2. No text, letters, numbers or watermark
3. No people
4. At most one gun fires, with its flash and smoke at the muzzle and its ball on the barrel's line. The flak's burst counts as its one shot
5. Every cannon is complete, one barrel on one carriage, not floating, not pasted, not buried in a wall
6. Every gun stands on the courtyard floor inside the walls, never on a wall, a wall walk, a tower, a roof or a house
7. Cannon barrels raised to about 45 degrees. The mortar points steeply up, the flak aims up
8. Castle walls straight and along the grid. Round corner towers are allowed
9. Where land shows, the tile grid is painted in the castles' perspective
10. Not too busy, nothing too small, not barren
11. The bottom right corner is calm where the loading bar sits, the lowest sixth across the right third: no important subject there
12. No siege gun, nothing bigger than the 2x2 heavy gun
13. Ideas 5 to 13: recognisably the cover's near castle, a square keep with a timber lookout under an orange pyramid roof and a red flag

What each image is meant to show:
Image 1 (idea 11, The castle after the battle): The castle after a battle, seen from high above at a three-quarter angle: thin smoke rises from two spots, a stretch of its front wall is broken with cream stone blocks lying in the grass below it, a dark scorched crater in the field, the red flag still flies on the keep.
Image 2 (idea 12, The castle grown): The castle grown larger, seen from high above at a three-quarter angle: a second straight ring of walls now surrounds the first, with more houses, a timber yard of stacked logs and two more cannons in the new outer courtyard, a road and a bridge leading to the gate.
Image 3 (idea 13, The castle after rain): The castle as a light rain clears, seen from high above at a three-quarter angle: wet shining roofs and stones, puddles on the paths, grey-blue clouds breaking up, a soft rainbow over the river.
Image 4 (idea 14, A wall is hit): A close view, seen from above at a three-quarter angle, of one straight stretch of cream castle wall with battlements at the moment a cannonball strikes it: stone blocks burst outward from the impact, chips and a round cloud of dust fly, the rest of the wall stands. Behind the wall orange roofs and a square keep, in front of it green grass with the tile grid.
Image 5 (idea 15, The breach): Seen from high above at a three-quarter angle: a castle whose straight front wall has a gap three blocks wide after a hit, cream stone blocks scattered in the grass in front of it, dust settling, and through the gap the courtyard with its cannons. A distant castle with a blue flag across the river in the upper part of the picture.

For each image 1 to 5, list only the checklist lines that FAIL, each with its number and one short reason naming where in the image. Also name anything that contradicts what the image is meant to show. If an image passes everything, write PASS. A line you cannot check counts as a FAIL. End with one line that starts with FAILS: followed by the numbers of the images with at least one failing line, or NONE.
