Pictures 5 to 13 show the cover's near castle in one place at different times, seasons and stages. They keep the cover's river and path on purpose. Every other picture has its own landscape.

The loading bar moves to the bottom right corner of the screen. In the castle pictures the castle's front corner sits where a centred bar would cover it, and the bottom right corner is calm in all twenty.

Critics: Gemini and GLM read all twenty, then the eleven changed pictures again, see [critique/](critique). They found the stray ruin in 7, the cannons on a raised wall top in 14 and 17, and the missing second storey in 16. They missed the two cannons outside the gates in 18. I checked every other claim in a crop and overrule it:

- **Barrels at 15 to 20 degrees** (GLM on 6 to 9, Gemini on 7 and 15). In the crops they rise 30 to 40 degrees, as in the cover art.
- **Something important under the bar** (11, 12, 15, 16, 18). The bar's real box drawn on each picture shows it clear.
- **Not the cover's castle** (on 1 to 3 and 15). Line 13 covers pictures 5 to 13 only.
- **The flak has two barrels** (GLM). That is its design (D60).
- **Two guns fire in 18** (Gemini). One fires.
- **The ball in 20 is far from the barrel** (Gemini). Its trail runs from the muzzle to the ball, which is the idea.

One open point: the cannon barrels rise about 30 to 40 degrees in this view, like the cover art's. If you want them steeper, name the pictures and I fix them through masks.
