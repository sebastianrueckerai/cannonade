# Round 29: concept art for the loading screen

Sebastian, 2026-10-15: "Just make a few more pictures in the same style as the cover art. A cannon firing, different version of one castle or a wall being shot... come up with 20 ideas, make them into images, critique them, refine them". The pictures go behind the loading bar (D65), one at random per load, so each must read on its own at 1920 by 1080 with a panel over its bottom middle.

Four groups: guns firing (1 to 5, the plain cannon, the heavy gun, the mortar and the flak of the game, D31 and D60, no siege gun, D64), one castle in many versions (5 to 13, the near castle of the cover art), a wall being shot (14 to 17), the world and the game (18 to 20).
