Painted sketch sheet edit. The three toadstools on this sheet are far too big: each stands about two and a half times as tall as the cannon, and they must be small. Repaint each toadstool at about 40 percent of its current painted size, the same toy style, the same thick cream stalks, the same wide rounded caps with their pale band and lighter dots, the same three cap colours (left warm red-orange, middle chestnut brown, right pale sandy-tan), each still standing centred on its own square grass tile, so each toadstool is clearly smaller than the cannon, its cap at about the height of the cannon's barrel. Keep everything else exactly as it is: the painting style, the warm cream paper, the grass tiles, the small dark iron cannon on its square plate unchanged in size, the soft shadows and light.
