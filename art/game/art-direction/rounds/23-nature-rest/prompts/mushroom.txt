The sheet shows three stylized toadstools for a toy fortress game, side by side in one row, each standing on its own square grass tile one metre wide, seen in a three-quarter view from slightly above. The tiles lie loose on the cream paper like game pieces, their edges drawn as a slightly darker green line, so the size of each toadstool reads against its tile. Every toadstool has the same shape: a thick cream stalk and a wide rounded cap with a pale band under it and a few small lighter dots on top, about a third of a tile wide and just under half a tile tall. The three toadstools differ only in their cap colours: Left: a warm red-orange cap with cream dots. Middle: a chestnut brown cap with soft cream dots. Right: a pale sandy-tan cap with soft cream dots. In front of the middle object, on its own tile, stands a small dark iron cannon on a low square plate that fills its tile: it is the size reference of the game. The toadstool must read as much smaller than the cannon.
