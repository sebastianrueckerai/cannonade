You are judging built 3D game assets for a cozy toy-fortress artillery game seen from above at 60 degrees. The image is one gate board. Rows, top to bottom: the mortar's picked painted sketch and three renders with its barrel at -10, +45 and +80 degrees; the flak's sketch and three renders with its gun cluster level, at +43 and straight up; five side close-ups of the flak's ball joint from 0 to 90 degrees; two zoomed in-game frames; two straight-down in-game frames; magnified in-game crops of a cluster bomb leaving the mortar and of a falling shard, then renders of the cluster bomb, the shard and the existing cannonball; two 3x crops of the default game view.

The requirements, in the brief's own words:
- Mortar, cannon22b, 2x2: "a squat wide-mouthed iron mortar in a heavy iron bed on the round turning mount, tier 2 industrial. A sidegrade of cannon22a that reads as a different gun at the default view: a short fat tube at 45 degrees against the long gun." Parts: a barrel that tilts about its trunnion pivot, a square plate under the gun. 1,500 triangles with the plate.
- Flak, flak22, 2x2: "a twin or quad mount of thin barrels pointed at the sky, tier 2 to 3, dark iron, on the round mount. It must not read as a wall gun." The whole gun cluster turns and tilts as one part about the ball. 1,500 triangles with the plate.
- Cluster bomb, 0.3 m: "a rounder, banded iron ball that reads apart from the 0.28 m shell". Shard, 0.12 m: "a chunk of iron". Under 100 triangles each.
- Every gun's main colour is dark iron grey. Every gun stands on a square plate in the gun's grey that stays square to the grid.

Name only faults you can point to in the panels: a moving part that leaves pieces behind or floats off its pivot when it tilts, parts passing through each other at any pose shown, a gun off-centre on its plate, the mortar reading like the long cannon, the flak reading like a wall gun, doubled or missing barrels, smeared or wrong-coloured textures, a projectile that cannot be told from the cannonball, holes in the mesh. For each fault name the row and panel and where in it. If a panel looks correct, do not invent faults. End with VERDICT: PASS or VERDICT: FAIL.
