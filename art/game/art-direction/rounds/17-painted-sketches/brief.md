# Round 17: painted sketches of the guns and the resource blocks

Phase F again. Round 16 was rejected as a whole. This round starts from Sebastian's words, quoted unchanged below, and uses no image of round 16. Every sheet is checked against the brief line by line before he sees it.

## Sebastian's words, 12 October

On the guns:

> I need sketches of cannons, no wheels, turnable, dark iron, wood on the smaller models, no multi barrel, no mortars, standard artiellery style cannons, each visible better and more advanced then the lst, not just the same model upsized. Cozy, stlye like the reference images

On the resources:

> Ressoucres need to be block / block shaped to they are visiuzally easy to see and drop into square fileds. They also need to be stacklable. Hence the box shape.

On the style:

> make sure the style of the concept images and the czy, bright, steam pills is taken into consideration. We want SKETCHES of things, not yet exact templates in 2d for 3d images. Its PAINTING, not 2d representations of low poly renders.

Earlier comments that still hold (D28, D29):

- One gun per footprint size: 1 by 1, 2 by 2, 3 by 3.
- No platform. The gun is the attraction.
- A round shield suits a big gun. A small half shield, perhaps with a visor slit, suits a smaller one.
- The four blocks: a crate with log ends at its corners, stacked stone blocks, iron ingots in two layers of three with the upper layer turned 90 degrees and a stepped pyramid on top, an open crate with one big cog. All the same box size.

## The checklist for every gun sheet

1. It is a painting: visible brushwork, painted light. It does not look like a 3D render or low poly.
2. Cozy and bright, like the references.
3. Three guns, small to large.
4. Each gun is a classic cannon with exactly one barrel. No multiple barrels, no mortar.
5. No wheels anywhere. Each gun sits on a round turning mount.
6. No platform or base slab.
7. Dark iron grey is the main colour of every gun.
8. Wood only on the smaller guns.
9. Each gun is visibly better and more advanced than the one before, not the same model scaled up.
10. No text, no labels.

## The checklist for every block sheet

1. A painting, cozy and bright.
2. Every block is a box of the same size.
3. The four blocks above, one each.
4. The iron is a box of crossed ingot layers with a pyramid on top, with no wooden box.
5. No text, no labels.
