You are an independent critic for a low-poly browser strategy game in the spirit of Rampart: castles on a tile grid shoot at each other with cannons. These are eight candidates for the main painted 2D illustration of the store page, without the title, which is added later as an overlay. Your job is faults, not taste.

The owner's checklist, every line must pass:
1. Castles shoot at castles; ships are not the subject.
2. The world seen from above.
3. Exactly one cannon fires, with a big smoke plume and a visible ball.
4. The firing barrel points where the ball flies: the flash and the smoke at the muzzle, the ball on the barrel's line.
5. Every cannon stands inside the walls on the courtyard floor; never on a wall, a wall walk, a tower, a roof or a house.
6. Every cannon is complete: one barrel on one wooden carriage with wheels, not floating, not pasted, not buried in a wall.
7. The barrels raised to about 45 degrees, so they shoot over the wall.
8. All cannons the same size, allowing for distance.
9. Each castle has two cannons; the two castles are not mirror images.
9b. The two castles in one image clearly different: a different keep, different buildings, a different wall and tower layout.
10. The tile grid aligned with the castles, its lines parallel to the walls.
11. Not too busy, nothing too small.
12. Painterly, soft colours, not oversaturated, not a 3D render look.
13. No text anywhere.

Image 1 is the picks sheet, eight images numbered 1 to 8. Image 2 shows the far castle of each at three times the size. Image 3 shows the near castle of each and the shot at twice the size. Judge lines 3 to 9 from images 2 and 3.

For each image 1 to 8, list only the lines that FAIL, each with one short reason naming where in the image. If an image passes every line, write PASS. A line you cannot check counts as a FAIL. End with one line that starts with FAILS: followed by the numbers of the images with at least one failing line, or NONE.
