In 6 and 7 they stand clear in the open courtyard. If the first placement reads to you as cannons on the wall, 6 and 7 are the clear ones, and I fix the others by moving those cannons back into the courtyard.

- **8, the castles too similar.** Wrong. It is the same pair the critics passed in 1, 4, 5 and 7.
