# Key-art checklist

Every point Sebastian made on the key art, rounds 07 to 09, as a test. An image is shown only when it passes every line, each checked in a crop: the near castle at 2x, the far castle at 4x, the shot at 2x. A line that cannot be checked counts as a fail.

| # | rule | from | how it is checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Castles shoot at castles. Ships are not the subject | round 07 sheet 2 | whole frame |
| 2 | The world seen from above, where the game takes place | round 07 sheet 1 | whole frame |
| 3 | Exactly one cannon fires, with a big smoke plume and a visible ball | round 07 sheet 2, round 09 | the shot crop |
| 4 | The firing barrel points where the ball flies: the flash and the smoke at the muzzle, the ball on the barrel's axis | round 07 sheets 3 and 4 | the shot crop |
| 5 | Every cannon stands inside the walls on the courtyard floor. Never on a wall, a wall walk, a tower, a roof or a house | rounds 08 and 09 | near castle 2x, far castle 4x |
| 6 | Every cannon is complete: one barrel on one wooden carriage with wheels, not floating, not pasted, not buried in a wall | round 07 sheet 4, round 09 | near castle 2x, far castle 4x |
| 7 | The barrels raised to about 45 degrees, so they shoot over the wall | round 08 | near castle 2x, far castle 4x |
| 8 | All cannons the same size | round 08 | both castles |
| 9 | Each castle has its cannons; the two castles are the same style and not mirror images | round 07 sheet 4 | whole frame |
| 9b | The two castles clearly different: a different keep in shape, roof and height, different buildings, a different wall and tower layout, nothing the same at the same side | round 10 | both castles side by side |
| 10 | The tile grid aligned with the castles: its lines parallel to the walls, in the same perspective | round 07 sheet 4, rounds 08 and 09 | whole frame |
| 10b | Grid designs only: every castle wall is straight and runs along the grid, no round or curved walls. Round corner towers are allowed | round 11 | whole frame |
| 11 | Not too busy, nothing too small | round 07 sheet 2 | whole frame |
| 11b | Not barren: the land carries trees, rocks, paths or water around the castles, not a bare field | round 11 | whole frame |
| 12 | Painterly, soft colours, not oversaturated, not the game's render look | round 07 sheet 1 | whole frame |
| 13 | No text in a main image; the title comes after the OK, and every image on a sheet is the same in this | round 09 | whole frame |
| 14 | Main images only, no capsule work until the OK | round 08 | the sheet |
