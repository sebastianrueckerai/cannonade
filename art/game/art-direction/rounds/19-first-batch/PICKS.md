# Round 19: the first batch, phase I

Session 07. Painted sketches for the classes of the first batch that had no design yet (D33), in the painting style of the key art (D30). Each board was checked in magnified crops before Sebastian saw it, no critic ran.

| board | image | what it shows | faults found in crops |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [walls-1-ledge.png](walls-1-ledge.png) | the four wall shapes at both heights: square merlons, a low ledge at the foot | the tee and the cross drawn wrongly, the walls thinner than a third of a tile |
| 2 | [walls-2-cornice.png](walls-2-cornice.png) | merlons on a band that projects from both faces, no ledge | the walls as thick as towers |
| 3 | [walls-3-walkway.png](walls-3-walkway.png) | two rows of small merlons with a plank walkway, a darker band at the foot | none |

| 5 | [base.png](base.png) | four bases with royal blue team parts | design 4 has stones drawn on its walls |

The image model drew the shapes as sketches. The sizes of the walls come from the game data, so the pick was the look, not the shapes.

## Picks (D38)

- **Walls:** board 2, the cornice. Boards 1 and 3 were the candidates for looks 2 and 3, which Sebastian dropped (D43).
- **Depot:** all four designs, for variety, as `supply` to `supply.4`. The reference views gave designs 3 and 4 orange tile roofs, and every depot got flat roof planes, because Meshy shredded raised tiles.
- **Base:** designs 1, 3 and 4, as `base` to `base.3`, not the round tower. The reference view of design 4 has plain walls.
- **Blocks:** the 600-triangle Meshy runs of the timber crate and the iron ingots, from views of [resource_blocks.png](../../anchors/resource_blocks.png).

After the first screenshots in the game: the walls passed, every gun got a square plate (D39), and the base became 2x2 (D40).
