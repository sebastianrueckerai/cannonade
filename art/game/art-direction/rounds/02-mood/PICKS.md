# Picks, round 02: mood and theme

Four candidate worlds, in words in [themes.md](themes.md), one board each: [board-toy-harbour.jpg](board-toy-harbour.jpg), [board-lighthouse-islands.jpg](board-lighthouse-islands.jpg), [board-river-town.jpg](board-river-town.jpg), [board-shipwright-yard.jpg](board-shipwright-yard.jpg). Every board holds six frames: coast, fortress from the game camera, harbour at another time of day, battle, material study, sky. All 24 in [contact-sheet.jpg](contact-sheet.jpg) and on [index.html](index.html). The Steam shots that fit each candidate are in `refs-<slug>.jpg`.

Critics: Gemini ranked toy-harbour, lighthouse-islands, river-town, shipwright-yard. GLM ranked toy-harbour, river-town, shipwright-yard, lighthouse-islands. Both put toy-harbour first for readability at 13 pixels, and both name the same merge as better than any single board: toy-harbour's chunky readable forms and clean materials with river-town's warm colours, half-timber and orange tile roofs. The director disagrees and ranks lighthouse-islands, river-town, toy-harbour, shipwright-yard, see [notes.md](notes.md). Your pick is the decision and goes into [docs/decisions.md](../../../../docs/decisions.md).
