Sheet 4, [picks-sheet-4.jpg](picks-sheet-4.jpg), labelled 20a to 25, is made on the comments to sheet 3: the land map of 12 with its cannons redrawn, 11 with cannons on both islands, 13 with the shot on the barrel's line, and new land maps, every prompt ending in the same cannon physics. [capsule-small-4.png](capsule-small-4.png) shows them at 460 and 231 px wide, the Steam header capsule and the small capsule, magnified twice. The corrected cross-domain table is [surfaces.md](surfaces.md), it becomes chapter 5 of the bible. The pick sets the painted style of the store, the splash and the menu, and the lettering of the title, together; the picked image becomes the marketing anchor, and the menu and the splash frames are made from it in its style.

## Comments

Sebastian, 12 October, on sheet 4, with 2x crops of the cannons: in 20a and 20b the cannon hovers in the air over a house; in 21 the front wheel of a cannon is buried in the wall; 25 has two broken cannons. 25 is a nice concept to develop along the others, but its grid is rotated 90 degrees against the castles, the castles are seen from the side while the grid stays isometric. 22's firing cannon still does not point where its ball flies. The cannons shooting are the main thing in the game: they cannot point in a direction other than their projectile's, and they have to look over the walls. In symmetric setups the two castles must not be mirror images; the same style, different buildings. The director's "right" verdicts on this sheet were wrong: they were made on the full frames at screen size, without a crop. Sheet 5 adds a crop step before any verdict.
