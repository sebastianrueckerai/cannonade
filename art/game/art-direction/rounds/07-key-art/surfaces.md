# What stays and what differs, take two

Phase E after D24. Round 06 edited every surface from the world anchor and was rejected: the 2D surfaces are a separate painted style that shares the theme. The table below replaces the one in [06-surfaces/surfaces.md](../06-surfaces/surfaces.md). The world and the UI rows keep the bible's chapters 2 to 4. The menu, the splash, the store and the trailer rows are painted 2D illustration, judged against the capsule references in [inspirations/](../../inspirations), not against the world anchor.

**What stays everywhere.** The theme: the toy fortress with cream walls, square battlements and orange roofs, bronze cannons, ships with striped sails, a blue sea, a warm afternoon. The motifs: crenellations like teeth, flags, barrels, round trees. The one lockup: the title is drawn once and used on every surface. The world's hues as the painter's starting palette.

**What differs.** The rendering. The game is low poly in the engine, from the rules of chapters 2 to 4. Every 2D surface is a painted illustration: brushwork, soft edges, the painter's light and shapes. A painting never pretends to be a screenshot, and a screenshot on the store is never painted over. The references for the painted style are the capsules in [inspirations/](../../inspirations): River Towns, Foundation, Kingdoms and Castles, Flotsam, Fabledom, A Monster's Expedition, and from D24 My Time at Portia and Doloc Town.

**Typography.** The title is judged inside a key-art candidate, never on its own. Each candidate of this round carries the lockup in the painting, in its own lettering, so the pick sets the painted style and the lettering together. The pick fills [typography.yaml](../../typography.yaml); the SVG of D1 is drawn from it. The UI font stays one family for every screen, readable at 16 px.
