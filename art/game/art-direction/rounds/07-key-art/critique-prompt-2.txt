You are an independent critic for a low-poly browser strategy game in the spirit of Rampart: a painted toy fortress on a tile grid, cheerful, chunky, sunlit, cream stone, orange roofs, green grass, yellow sand, blue sea, striped sails, and cannons, which are the heart of the game. This phase decides the painted 2D key art of the store page, which is a separate style from the game's render and shares its theme, and with it the lettering of the title. Your job is faults and readability, not taste.

Image 1 is the first sheet of this round, four candidates labelled 2 to 5, which the owner rejected with this feedback: 2 a bit too cartoony and stylised, the font is bad, the lighting and the colours are good; 3's title is invisible, light on light, the colours washed out, but the painted outline idea is nice and the painting should fill the frame; 4 has good colours and the best title, readable and clean, but the world should be seen from above, where the game takes place, and it has no cannons; 5 is too saturated and too close to the game's render, not painterly enough, though its tiles are nice.
Image 2 is the second sheet, four new candidates labelled 6 to 9, made on that feedback: 6 the view from above with the cannons firing; 7 the same view in a gouache look with painted outlines; 8 a closer view with one cannon big in the foreground; 9 candidate 4 reworked with the camera raised, cannons added and a tile grid.
Image 3 is the capsule size test of the second sheet: each candidate at 460 and 231 pixels wide, the Steam header capsule and the small capsule, magnified twice.

Answer in this order, short and concrete:
1. Per candidate 6 to 9: does it answer the feedback, point by point: seen from above, cannons as the main subject, the title readable and clean at 231 px on a quiet sky, painterly and not a render, colours good and not oversaturated, a tile grid visible. Name what is still missing.
2. The title, per candidate: every letter correct and well formed, the size, the contrast against what is behind it. Name any malformed letter.
3. Faults: anything that is not the game's theme, anything the prompt did not ask for, any object that is wrong, such as a cannon that points the wrong way or a ship out of scale.
4. Rank the four, how fully they answer the feedback first, readability of the title at the small capsule second, one sentence each. End with one line that starts with RANKING: followed by the four numbers in order.
