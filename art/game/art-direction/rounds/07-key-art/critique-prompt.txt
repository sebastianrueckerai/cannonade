You are an independent critic for a low-poly browser strategy game in the spirit of Rampart: a painted toy fortress on a tile grid, cheerful, chunky, sunlit, cream stone, orange roofs, green grass, yellow sand, blue sea, striped sails. This phase decides the painted 2D key art of the store page, the splash and the menu, which is a separate style from the game's render and shares its theme, and with it the lettering of the title. Your job is faults and readability, not taste.

Image 1 is the pattern, six Steam capsules of other games: My Time at Portia, Doloc Town, Fabledom, Tiny Glade, The Wandering Village, the Ancient Egypt DLC of TerraScape. Each is a painting, whatever the game renders, and each title is white or cream, large, clear at capsule size, on a quiet part of the painting, in a script that carries the game's mood.
Image 2 is the picks sheet, four key-art candidates for GRID CANNONS, labelled 2 chunky, 3 storybook, 4 painted script, 5 painted storybook. Candidate 1 was blocked by the image model's safety filter three times and does not exist.
Image 3 is the capsule size test: each candidate scaled to 460 and 231 pixels wide, the Steam header capsule and the small capsule, magnified twice without smoothing.

Answer in this order, short and concrete:
1. The lettering, per candidate: is every letter correct and well formed, does the title read at 231 px, does its script feel cheerful and cosy or something else, and does it sit on a quiet part of the painting. Name any malformed letter.
2. The painting, per candidate: is it a 2D painting or does it still look like a 3D render; does it show the game's theme, the fortress with cream walls and orange roofs, cannons, a ship with striped sails; what is missing or wrong; does the composition work as a Steam header capsule at 460 by 215, where the sides get cropped.
3. Which candidate follows the pattern of image 1 most closely, and in what, and which breaks it, and in what.
4. For the one you rank first: one correction to the lettering and one to the painting that would make it a finished capsule.
5. Rank the four, readability of the title at the small capsule first, fit to the pattern and to the game's theme second, one sentence each. End with one line that starts with RANKING: followed by the four numbers in order.
