## Lessons for the tools

- `ideogram/v4` blocks an innocent prompt at random: the filter judges the generated image, the same prompt passes or fails from run to run. Three blocks on one prompt means switch models, not reword.
- `ideogram/v4` misspelled a two-word title once in five runs. Read every title before it reaches a sheet.
- `google/nano-banana-2.1/edit` wrote a two-word title correctly seventeen times when capsule references or a frame with the title were attached. Its text fault from the probe is on many small words, a menu, not on a title. The providers page says so now.
- The world anchor as the last reference pulls the painting toward the render: 5 kept the anchor's layout and shading with two painted references, 4 became a painting with three. The balance is the number of painted references against the one render.
- A lettering style survives an edit of the same image (9 kept 4's title) and does not survive a new composition from references (6 got a cursive from "hand-lettered script"). To carry a title into a new painting, attach the frame that has it: with 4 attached as the lettering reference, seven of nine new compositions kept its script, and the two that did not, 10 and 14, took the cursive of the first reference, 6.
- "One cannon fires" held in seven of ten. The two that fired twice, 14 and 19, were the two closest to 6, which fires twice; the reference's action leaks as much as its lettering.
