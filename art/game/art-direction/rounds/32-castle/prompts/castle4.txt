Paint it as a concept artist's painted sketch sheet for a cozy, bright strategy game: loose hand-painted brushwork with visible strokes, soft painted light from a warm sunny afternoon coming from the front right, cheerful bright colours, a warm light cream paper background with a soft painted shadow under each object. It is a painting, not a 3D render, not low poly, not a game screenshot. Cozy and friendly like a storybook toy, never grim. No people, no text, no labels, no numbers, no letters, no watermark.

The sheet shows four different designs for one building, side by side from left to right: the castle keep of a toy fortress, the prize the walls are built around. Each design stands on a square base of exactly nine bright green grass tiles, three rows of three, and the building covers the base edge to edge: its outer walls sit directly on the border lines of the base, its footprint exactly as wide and as deep as the base, with no grass margin around the building. Each design is one single connected keep of warm cream stone, plain smooth walls with no stones drawn on them: one building made of several joined volumes of clearly different sizes, one big main mass with smaller parts attached straight onto it, wings, annex blocks and small towers that share their walls with the main mass, every part touching the next with no gap anywhere, so together they read as one solid multi-part building and the small parts next to the big one convey its scale. No curtain wall, no courtyard, no fence, no gatehouse, no detached buildings of any kind. The keep is low and squat: clearly wider than it is tall, its height at most half the width of its base, with the attached parts even lower, so it never blocks the view of anything behind it. The main volumes are built in two or three low storeys with a clear horizontal band between storeys, so whole parts and storeys could crumble away on their own. It carries a few small details that convey scale: an arched wooden entrance door with steps, one or two chimneys, small dormer windows set into roofs, buttresses at the corners of the main mass, a wooden awning over one door. It does not carry many flags and not a regular grid of windows. The team colour is a plain royal blue: on every design the roofs and the flags are royal blue, and nothing else is blue. Design 1: the classic bergfried, one big square main tower with battlements on top, a small round stair turret attached at one corner rising just above the main roof, and one low rectangular annex with its own small pitched roof at its foot, one royal blue flag. Design 3: the chunky toy keep, a big square two storey main hall with battlements, four small corner turrets with tiny royal blue pyramid roofs of slightly different heights, and one lower attached wing with dormers and a chimney. Design 4: the broad hall keep, one wide low main hall with a stepped gable along its front, a cross wing attached at the back reaching to the edge of the base, the wing with its own ridge and dormers, one small round turret on the corner where hall and wing meet no taller than the roof, chimneys on both parts, a royal blue flag on the turret.
