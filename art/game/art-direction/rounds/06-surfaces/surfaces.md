# What stays and what differs

Phase E, written before the images. One row per surface, one column per thing a surface could re-decide. The world is the reference: the first four columns are the bible's chapters 2 to 4 and hold everywhere. Only composition, the lighting on the store and the text change per surface. The three frames of this round show the menu, the splash and the store still edited from [world_style.png](../../anchors/world_style.png), so the only change in each is the one the table allows.

**What stays everywhere.** The palette, the shapes, the detail cap and the motifs: striped sails, crenellations like teeth, flags, barrels, round trees, the chamfer. A surface that needs a colour the palette lacks sends the palette back to phase B. A surface that needs more detail than River Towns sends the fault back to phase C.

**What differs, and the only reason it may.** Composition, because the gameplay camera is the wrong angle for a menu and the wrong distance for a splash. Lighting on the store, because the store needs a second look and the evening light is the mood the game itself rejected for readability. Text, because the world has none and the store has copy.

**Typography.** Three title studies set the direction: carved wood, painted sign, naval brass. The pick fills [typography.yaml](../../typography.yaml). The UI font is one family for every screen, readable at 16 px on 1280 by 720 and at phone width. The title is a lockup, not a font: the SVG of D1 is drawn from the picked study.

**The three frames.** Menu: the hero angle, the title zone open. Splash: the mood painting. Store: the drama. Each edited from the world anchor, so what stays is visible and what differs is the single change the table names.
