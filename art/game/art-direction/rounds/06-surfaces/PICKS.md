## Comments

Sebastian, 11 October. The titles: 2 is the best of the three and all three are not good. A font cannot be judged in isolation, it has to sit on a capsule advertising the game. 2 looks grimy, like a horror game. 3 looks like Age of Empires, not fun, stiff. 1 looks plain bad and not like wood, like old-school WordArt. The frames: none, they all look terrible. The round used the game's render look for the 2D art, and that is not how it works. The references show it: the splash screens and above all the store art are 2D, more painterly, a separate art style that uses the game's theme, maybe its colours and its elements. Round 07 redoes the phase on that premise.
