# Picks, round 34: the win and lose screens

Six pictures, three a side, painted in the loading-screen style (D66 voice) with [nano-banana 2.1 edit](https://fal.ai/models/nano-banana-2.1/edit) at 0.15 USD each. Every picture carries two references: the picked loading look [refs/style.png](refs/style.png) for the style ("take only the soft painterly 2D look, the colours and the light of the first attached image"), and a render of the game's own model for the shape ([refs/castle.png](refs/castle.png) healthy, [refs/ruin.png](refs/ruin.png) the d4 ruin). The win prompts call the style ref's pink roof a placeholder and ask for royal blue; the lose prompts want the torn royal blue banner on the rubble and the enemy castle whole with dark red roofs and banners. Shared rules: the painted tile grid on the land, straight cream walls with corner towers, the subject off-centre, the middle calm for the title and the buttons, no people, no text, no 3D-render sheen.

| # | the picture | the critic's word |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | win, hilltop: the keep above morning fields, the enemy a smoking ruin across a foggy valley | 2nd: calm fields, the fog separates the castles well |

| 6 | lose, dusk: the ruin on a headland at dusk, one fire still glowing in the rubble | 3rd: the campfire asks a story question the screen should not ask |
