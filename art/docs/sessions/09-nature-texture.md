# Session 09: The texture decision and the nature look

Start prompt for the session: "Run session 09 from docs/sessions/09-nature-texture.md. Go."

Roadmap items: B6 (flat palette or textured, decided on variants of one asset), C5 (the nature set through its gate), F7 finished for good (the game-repo nature state decided).

## Why these deliverables

- **The texture decision (B6).** D46 rejected the nature rebuild with "Its also still untextured." The vertical gradient material, painted for the D41 storybook look, reads as a flat colour at game size. B6 was always going to be decided "after C2 with both variants of one asset side by side"; the gate failure is that moment.
- **The nature look (C5).** The nine procedural solids stand structurally (D45: closed, solid, byte-identical rebuilds) but no look passed. Nature covers the most board after the walls and the tile; the game cannot look finished with it flat grey.
- **The game-repo nature state.** Nine `nature_*.glb` sit untracked in the game repo, and the manifest no longer lists them (D46). The next gate pass replaces them and commits them; a second rejection re-runs the sync from the new source.

## Plan

1. **B6, both variants of one asset.** The leaf tree twice from the same mesh: flat palette colours against a generated texture (paint overshoot, soft brush noise, the storybook gradient baked in). Both in magnified Blender views and in 3x in-game crops, side by side, one board. Sebastian picks; the decision entry closes B6.
2. **C5, the look round on the picked variant.** Three looks per kind from the D44 palettes, painted as small reference sheets first if the texture needs one. Gate board: magnified close-ups per the standing rules, the whole board viewed by the model before Sebastian sees it.
3. **On pass: sync and commit.** `asset.py approve` the nine, `godot_sync.py --force`, reimport, `check_art.tscn` clean, the game-repo commit lands the nature GLBs and their manifest entries.
4. **Close.** Decision entries, process-log lines, roadmap ticks (B6, C5, F7), the session 10 plan.

## Limits

- At most 25 new images shown per round (D19). Sketches are paintings, never in the low-poly render look (D30).
- Reproducibility: every texture is generated by a seeded script with a sidecar JSON, same as meshes.
- Godot runs `--headless` or under `xvfb-run`, Blender runs `--background`.
- Game changes land in the game repo on `master`, limited to what this plan names. Sebastian's uncommitted edits there stay untouched.
- Spending cap 14 USD in `.env.session` unless session 09's start rewrites it.

## Done when

B6 has its decision entry. The nine nature assets pass their gate on the picked variant, stand in the game through the art layer, and the game repo holds them committed. The session 10 plan is written.
