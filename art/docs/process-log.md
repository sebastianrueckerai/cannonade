Lessons from the models:

- **Meshy shreds raised roof tiles.** At 550 triangles the curved tiles of two depots came out as spikes with holes. The fault went back to the reference: views that paint the tiles on two flat planes gave clean roofs on all four depots.
- **A compact item gets a three-quarter view,** which shows its top.
- **The browser build needs small textures.** Meshy's 2,048 px PNG textures made the first batch 11 MB. Scaled to 1,024 px for the guns and 512 px for the rest and stored as JPEG, it is 1.5 MB, with no change in a 2x crop.
- **The team part must read at the default view.** A roof does. A flag and a banner alone barely do, which is open for base 2.

Lessons from the game:

- **The light needed a measured energy.** The rules give colours and angles but no energy. At 1.0 the cream walls rendered white. At 0.5 a lit top face shows its palette colour, and that number is now in [lighting_rules.yaml](../game/art-direction/lighting_rules.yaml). The rules file also defined the sun's azimuth from the wrong side: 40 degrees put the sun behind the board, while D23 and the anchor have it at the front right. The comment is fixed, the numbers stand.
- **Old assets react to new light.** Turning off the reflected light made the old metallic FBX nature props black, so it stays on. The props are still darker than the palette, a reason to restyle nature next.
- **A bigger base exposed an old AI bug.** The plan search read the fine flood-fill grid with tile coordinates, a bug kept from the Unity original, so an AI's plan spots depended on an unrelated corner of the map. The 2x2 base closed the cells AI 4 happened to read, and AI 4 stopped building. Both reads now take the tile's centre cell, and the four-AI simulation passes. Two tests took new hand-derived counts for the 2x2 start layout, and the stacking test of the human-input check no longer depends on the random draw.

Sebastian's review after the first screenshots changed three things: every gun stands on a square plate in its own grey that stays on the grid while the gun turns (D39), the cooldown ring floats above the guns, and the base is a 2x2 building (D40). ## 2026-10-13 Session 09: the texture question settled by evidence, not by another render

B6 came to its board after the seam taught the method. The painted contact shadow between crown and trunk failed three vision verdicts in a row while pixel measurement said it was there: 27 percent row-mean contrast, a 156 px scalloped edge, the crease on the visible trunk. The fails were a presentation artifact. Asked at full 560 px the model smeared the band into a gradient; asked on the same render cropped to the trunk strip and magnified 2x it passed every criterion. Two rules follow, and both went onto the boards:

- A texture gate is asked at the scale the player sees it. The B6 and C5 boards carry magnified crops of the deciding region, not only whole views.

- Vision verdicts disagree with pixel measurement on smooth gradients, so measurement settles whether a feature exists and vision only how it reads.

Determinism was the second lesson. In-game A/B crops were impossible while every screenshot run rolled a fresh map (`randomize()` without `--seed`) and a fresh variant set (an unseeded `RandomNumberGenerator` in `Art.gd`). Six candidate crops framed conifers or nothing. Pinning `--seed=1` and parking or overwriting the variant GLBs made the pair pixel-identical but for the material. Any in-game comparison from now on pins the seed and the variant set first.
